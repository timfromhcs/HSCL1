# Brand rules

- **Name:** `HSCL1` (capital letters, digit one). Never HSCL, HSC-Linux or HCSL1.
- **Long form:** **Hypercharged Supreme Computing Layout 1** (owner decision, 2026-11-06). Written in full in the README, About screens, installer slideshow, `os-release`
  (`HSCL1_NAME_LONG`), the website, the handbook and `hscl about`. The letters are H-S-C-L-1; the "1" is the first layout.
- **Publisher and credit:** HCSMEDIA - "Made with love by hcsmedia".
- **The name is also a program:** `hscl1` and `hscl` are the same binary (`/usr/bin/hscl1 -> hscl`). Called with no arguments it prints an overview of the product and where to start.
  The Control Center is also launched as "HSCL1".
- **Never claimed:** HSCL1 is not Tails, not an official Kali release, not endorsed by Canonical, KDE, Kubuntu, Offensive Security or the Tails project, and not related to Microsoft or Apple;
  no Microsoft/Apple artwork, sounds or fonts are used; "Windows-11-like" describes behaviour (centred taskbar, start menu, snap layouts), not assets.
- **Logo:** original mark generated by `scripts/gen_brand.py` (hexagon with a "1"); use the dark/light/mono SVGs, keep clear space, do not recolour outside the tokens.
